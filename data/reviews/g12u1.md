Work copy: data/wip/g12u1.review.js (original untouched in data/units until the final `mv`).
Method: every question (grammar 18+18+21, reading 11, test 20 = 88) was solved cold before looking at the key; every mcq
option was tested as a possible second answer; every gap/tr list was checked against the app normaliser (U.norm: expands
n't/'m/'re/'ve/'ll, NOT 's/'d; ignores case, commas, final punctuation); every order item was tested for a second natural

gist/tr/gloss were checked paragraph by paragraph; the model blog post was counted; vocab headwords were checked against
their example sentences, definitions (≤ 14 words) and Arabic senses. The Sapir–Whorf article was fact-checked (see Reading).
