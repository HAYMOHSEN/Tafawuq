## Residual doubts
- grammar[0] qs[5] and test[8] (managed to vs could): they rely on the taught rule that affirmative single-occasion past success takes managed to / was able to, not could. Some native speakers tolerate "could … finally" in narrative, but it is the standard exam rule and "could" is the intended distractor; kept.
- reading.qs: in 5 of the 7 mcq the correct option is also the longest, and both tf items are false; not wrong, but test-wise students may spot the pattern (left as is to keep wording stable).
- writing model: "A majority of respondents (55%) complained that the seats are uncomfortable" paraphrases survey item 2 ("biggest problem"); acceptable for a model answer, though "named the seats as the biggest problem" would be stricter.
