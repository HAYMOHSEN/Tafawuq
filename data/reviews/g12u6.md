# Review log — g12u6 "Feels good" (independent review)

Work copy: data/wip/g12u6.review.js (original untouched in data/units until the final `mv`).

Method: every question (grammar 18+18+18, reading 11, test 20) was solved cold before looking at the key; every mcq
option was tested as a possible second answer; every gap/tr list was run through the app's normaliser (U.norm:
expands n't/'m/'re/'ve/'ll/can't/won't, NOT 's/'d; ignores case, commas and final punctuation); every order item
was tested for a second natural arrangement of the same tokens; every English example and its Arabic translation
was read line by line; reading gist/tr/gloss were checked paragraph by paragraph; the model article was counted
(244 words incl. title / 234 without; range 200–250); every Dead Sea statement was checked for accuracy and hedging;
the "\n" mcq stems (test 3, 5, 8, 14, 15, 16 and the poem) were checked against the renderer
(.q-text{white-space:pre-line} on screen, mdn() -> <br> in print) and read as lines.

Overall: a strong unit. All 85 answer keys were confirmed correct; no false grammar rule was found. The Arabic
explanations, tr and gist are accurate and fluent. Changes below are therefore few and mostly small.

## Changes

### Grammar 2 — Reported speech (questions)
- [critical] qs[17] order "He said he wished he had studied harder." — the shuffled tokens also make the natural,
  grammatical sentence "He had said he wished he studied harder." (studied = past simple or participle; three "he"),
  so the key was ambiguous → replaced by "He said he wished he had known the answer." (known can only be a participle,
  so only one order works); x reworded.
- [minor] qs[14] err "She asked me where was the nearest pharmacy." (w = was) — the app prints "w → c", and
  "was → the nearest pharmacy was" reads as "replace was by …", giving a duplicated noun phrase → c = "move it after
  “pharmacy” (where the nearest pharmacy was)". Key and x unchanged.
- [minor] qs[9] gap "Hala said she would start her revision ____." — "on the next day / on the following day /
  on the day after" are equally correct and were rejected → added.
- [minor] qs[12] tr "Shall I switch off the lights?" — the natural variant "switch the lights off" was not accepted →
  added the two variants (if / whether).

### Grammar 1 — Reporting verbs

## Facts checked (Dead Sea and others)
- The unit makes no "lowest point on Earth" claim. Salinity statements are accurate (the Dead Sea is roughly ten times
  saltier than the ocean: "much saltier"/"far saltier" is correct). "Shrinking / level falling" is well documented.
  "Very little/hardly any life", "said to be unusually clean", "thought to ease joint pain", "believed to be good for the
  skin", "said to filter some of the sun's rays … still wear sunscreen" are all hedged and appropriate. The 20-minute
  limit in test 14 matches usual advice. "Birds pass through the Jordan Valley every autumn" is true and unquantified.
- No invented statistics; emotional labour is correctly attributed to sociologists; reading/Arabic tr are faithful.

## Residual doubts
1. "congratulate sb on" (gr1 qs[3], qs[14], test 5): a few native speakers accept "congratulate for". Kept as the
   Cambridge/Oxford standard; every learner dictionary lists only "on".
2. Tap-the-word err items built on verb patterns (gr1 qs[14] "congratulated her for", qs[15] "explained me") also have a
   verb-swap repair (praised / told). The key targets the dependent word, which is the standard exam target and what the
   x explains; left as is.
3. Gap/tr keys follow the exam convention of always shifting time/place/tense (where I was going; the next day; there).
   The unit's own warn box correctly notes that same-day/same-place reports may keep "tomorrow/here"; the app's
   "my answer is also correct" override covers this.
4. UI only (not content): js/views.js line ~569 (mistakes-review list) renders q.q without white-space:pre-line, so the
   poem/dialogue stems appear as one run-on line there (still readable). One-line CSS fix:
   `.review-item .q { white-space: pre-line; }`. Practice, exam and print views are correct.
5. 40 vocabulary items (validator max 40, schema says 28–36) — left as is (counts must not be reduced).
