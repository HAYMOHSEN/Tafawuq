## Residual doubts
1. "Diminishing returns" in the reading is the everyday sense (each extra unit gives less benefit); strictly, the cake example is diminishing marginal utility and the revision example is diminishing marginal returns. Acceptable for a C1 learner text; no statement is false.
2. hope: the unit teaches "hope = It is hoped that only" as an exam convention (kept, hedged with «غالباً» / «في الامتحان»). Real English also has "is hoped to" occasionally; no question depends on it any more.
3. "convince sb to do" is accepted by modern dictionaries; the unit now says so, but the exam advice (persuade sb to do) is the Tawjihi tradition. No question tests it.
4. Test item "cajole sb into staying … by offering a free dinner": cajole is the only option that is right for both pattern and sense, but "entice" would fit even better (not offered).
5. gr3.qs[8] (err "to"): `c` is the mixed string "(احذف to) I must have my hair cut" (shown as «to → (احذف to) …»; the print worksheet says "Correct word:"). It works but is untidy; left as is.
6. Radio extract (test[16]) uses "coax" without an object ("they coax gently") — fine in speech, slightly elliptical.
