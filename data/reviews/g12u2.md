## Residual doubts
- gr1 qs[5] / qs[16] and test[5]: "No sooner … than" is the exam-board rule; "No sooner … when" does occur in real usage but
  is non-standard, so "when" is kept as the wrong option.
- gr1 qs[2]: the distractor "she knew" (non-inverted "Little she knew …") exists in older/poetic English; it is marked as the
  non-standard option because modern usage inverts after sentence-initial Little.
- gr1 qs[0]: "Never have I seen … before today" mixes present perfect with "before today"; acceptable, though a narrative
  "Never had I seen …" would be equally natural (not offered, so no ambiguity).
- gr2 qs[0]: distractor "That" ("That was Ranya who …") can be read as an identifying sentence in speech; kept because the
  cleft frame is explicit and it is the standard textbook distractor.
- gr2 body (rule أ): "do not use what/where/when after the highlighted element" is exam-oriented; informal English does say
  "It was in 1998 when …". Same for the "reason … because" warn (prescriptive, formal register).
- test[19]: "Realistically, no passenger can reasonably be expected …" is slightly redundant but correct.
- The Arabic translation of paragraph 1 makes the teacher female (معلمتها); the English is gender-neutral.
