## Residual doubts
- Reading p5: "It seems that each of her projects began with the same simple question: what is missing, and how can I help to provide it?" is the writer's interpretive device (hedged with "It seems"), not a documented quotation of Sabriye; Reading Q6 is built on it. Left as a literary inference, but a very strict policy reading could prefer removing the first-person wording.
- Reading simplifications of real facts: "Tibetology" (she studied Central Asian Studies with Tibetan/Tibetology); "co-founded Braille Without Borders" (the organisation was founded in 1998 as "Project for the Blind, Tibet" and renamed in 2002); "became blind at about twelve" (sight was lost gradually, totally blind by 12). All acceptable for a Grade 11 text.
- grammar[0] qs[13] tr (useful → WORTH): "It is useful to learn…" vs "It's worth learning…" is a loose paraphrase (useful ≈ worth the effort). Key lists the 4 reasonable forms; kept.
- grammar[1] qs[3] mcq "try ____ a little cumin and see what happens": "try to add" is not impossible in casual speech, but "and see what happens" makes the experiment sense (try + V-ing) the only natural reading. Kept.
- Tooling (not content): tools/validate.js requires file name == id, so `data/wip/g11u5.review.js` cannot be validated directly ("id must equal file name"); validated an identical copy named g11u5.js. Also U.closeMatch accepts a one-letter difference for typed answers longer than 6 characters, so e.g. "to reads" would be accepted as a "typo" of "to read" — an app-level leniency, not a content issue.
