## Residual doubts
1. App behaviour, not content: `U.closeMatch` (js/core.js) accepts one-letter slips as "typos" for answers longer than 6 characters, so
   *didn't used* (grammar[0].qs[7], key "did not use") and *on which* / *in which* (grammar[1].qs[7], qs[8]) earn credit with only a
   "watch your spelling" note. Suggest switching the tolerance off when the one-letter difference is a whole grammatical word/ending.
2. Three exercises are near-copies of explanation examples (grammar[1].qs[7] café/where, grammar[2].qs[5] factory/leaving,
   grammar[2].qs[10] Ali/breaking), so students can answer from memory; not wrong, left unchanged.
3. grammar[2].qs[9] and test[13] differ in leniency: "Finishing our lunch, …" is accepted (punctual verb, defensible) while "Spending all his money, …" is not.
4. grammar[2].qs[2] distractor "Finished the exam early, …" is non-standard in modern English (fine in some literary/dialect uses); judged safe.
5. Rose Maylie is Mrs Maylie's *adopted* "niece" in the novel; the English simplification is acceptable, the Arabic "ابنة أخيها" commits to a brother's daughter.
6. tools/validate.js requires id == file name, so it always reports one ERROR for `data/wip/<id>.review.js`; validation was run on an identically named copy and on the moved file.
